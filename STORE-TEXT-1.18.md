## 2. Google Play - full description (max 4,000 characters)

Fly through ice spikes, dodge drifting asteroids and weave between glowing coral. Sky Hop takes you through 9 original worlds, each with its own obstacles, music and colours, and 10,000 levels to climb!

Tap to hop, thread the gaps and keep your bird in the air. Easy to pick up, tricky to master, and a great pick for teens and up.

ONE BIRD THAT EVOLVES AS YOU FLY
Meet your fiery Phoenix. It starts as a tiny Hatchling and grows through 40 evolution stages as you reach higher levels. Its wings get bigger, its tail gets longer, a crest sprouts and its glow gets hotter. Later stages add cartoon toy machinery: gears, helicopter rotors, goggles and treads. Each stage hands you a free upgrade, and every stage has its own evolution card. Your Phoenix always flies with one fiery red-and-gold trail, and a built-in coin magnet that reaches farther the more it evolves.

A SKILL TREE YOU BUILD WITH COINS
• 18 skill branches: Dash, Shield, Shrink, Quick Recharge, Glide, Second Chance, Coin Boost, Coin Reach, Rocket Boost, Power & Talons, Pellet Burst, Rocket Pop, the Poof Toy, plus new toy-machine branches: Toy Popper, Rotor Lift, Rapid Rockets, Bonk Bounty and Crumble Touch
• Every skill you buy lights up another feather on your Phoenix's tail, brighter with each level
• Coins are earned by playing. Nothing in the skill tree costs real money

FRIENDLY HELPERS FOR THE TRICKY PARTS
• Pellet Burst: for about 30 seconds your bird sends out a stream of bright, rainbow-coloured pellets with sparks and zig-zag lightning. A couple of pellets give a cheeky hunter a cartoon "Bonk!" with stars, then it vanishes in a puff of colourful smoke and confetti, and you get coins
• Rocket Pop: about 30 homing toy rockets with fiery trails that bonk hunters and pop whatever they throw
• Pellets, rockets and the Poof Toy also crack pipes. After a few hits a pipe half crumbles away in a cartoon puff, opening a bigger gap and giving you a few coins
• Poof Toy: your bird lobs a smiling toy that lands with a big, colourful starburst of confetti, sparks and lightning. It dazes hunters, pops their nets and cracks pipes in a small area
• Helpers now turn up steadily and stack, so Pellets, Rockets and the Poof Toy are often active together
• Witch-hunters grow tougher as the worlds go by, from pointy hats to an Archwitch. Helpers make them vanish in a confetti poof
• Hunters come in many shapes and sizes and never hurt you once dazed. Helpers never appear on boss levels
• Every world's obstacles crack and crumble as helpers hit them, and in later worlds they grow back as small stumps

9 WORLDS, 9 KINDS OF OBSTACLES
• Snowy Peaks: faceted ice pillars with crystal spike tips
• Outer Space: cratered asteroid columns with glowing veins
• Coral Reef: bubbling coral columns
• Lava Land: cracked lava rock with glowing seams
• Pastel Skies: swirled pastel pillars
• Night City: neon-lit skyscrapers
• Sunset Desert, Blossom Valley and the classic Day Sky
A new world opens every 20 levels, and boss levels shake things up with spiked obstacles.

MORE TO ENJOY
• Dash, shield, shrink and ghost skills help you out of tight spots. The ghost can be used several times in a run and recharges as you fly
• A pause button with a 3-2-1 countdown when you come back, and the game pauses by itself when you leave the app
• Fly close to the edges to start a combo chain and boost your coin pickups
• Daily missions and a daily streak
• An end-of-run reward chest, always coins only
• A weekly leaderboard so you can compare high scores with other players
• Trophies to unlock as you reach new levels

GOOD TO KNOW
• Free to play. No real-money purchases; coins are earned by playing
• Contains ads. Banner ads appear on menu and game-over screens, never during a run. Video ads are optional and only play when you choose them
• Short sessions, one-thumb controls, original art and music

Hop in and see how far your Phoenix can fly. How many stages can you evolve through?

## Notes for Danny
- 1.18 adds: steady stacking helper pickups (pity timer, about 5-8 s apart), reliable hunter defeat, 4 hunter tiers in many shapes and sizes, colourful effects (rainbow pellets, rocket flame trails, lightning, starbursts, sparks), obstacles that crack and crumble in every world (later worlds regrow a small stump), multi-use Ghost (2-4 charges + recharge), one fixed fiery trail (Trails tab removed), a built-in coin magnet (Magnet skill branch removed, 18 branches now), and a Pause button (Resume / Quit to menu, 3-2-1 countdown, auto-pause). Checkpoints (the "⚑ Checkpoint saved" line, the HUD flag and the watch-ad continue point) are now every 20 levels instead of every 10; boss levels stay every 10th level. A continue after a game over now restarts at most 19 levels back (was 9), so the continue offer is first available after level 20. Hunters appear naturally only from level 35.
- LEGAL: (1) hunters are poofed away instead of only stunned and have new looks (pointy hats, brooms, colours); (2) NEW: fire and lightning visuals (rocket flame trails, fiery impact bursts, zig-zag lightning arcs, starbursts) are all cartoon-style and capped, with no blood or bodies, but re-check the Play / itch content-rating questionnaire (mild cartoon fantasy violence) and get LEGAL sign-off before submitting.
