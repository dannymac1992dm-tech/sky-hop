## 2. Google Play - full description (max 4,000 characters)

Fly through ice spikes, dodge drifting asteroids and weave between glowing coral. Sky Hop has 9 original worlds, each with its own obstacles, music and colours, and 10,000 levels to climb!

Tap to hop, thread the gaps and keep your bird in the air. Easy to pick up, tricky to master, and a great pick for teens and up.

ONE BIRD THAT EVOLVES AS YOU FLY
Meet your fiery Phoenix. It starts as a tiny Hatchling and grows through 40 evolution stages as you reach higher levels. Its wings grow, its tail lengthens and its glow gets hotter. Later stages add cartoon toy machinery: gears, rotors, goggles and treads. Each stage hands you a free upgrade and has its own evolution card. Your Phoenix always flies with one fiery red-and-gold trail. Grab a Magnet pickup during a run and coins fly to you for a few seconds, with a soft pulsing ring that shows its reach.

A SKILL TREE YOU BUILD WITH COINS
• 18 skill branches: Dash, Shield, Shrink, Quick Recharge, Glide, Second Chance, Coin Boost, Coin Reach, Rocket Boost, Power & Talons, Pellet Burst, Rocket Pop, Poof Toy, Toy Popper, Rotor Lift, Rapid Rockets, Bonk Bounty and Crumble Touch
• Every skill you buy lights up another feather on your Phoenix's tail, brighter with each level
• Coins are earned by playing. Nothing in the skill tree costs real money

FRIENDLY HELPERS FOR THE TRICKY PARTS
A new helper turns up every 7-8 seconds or so. You get one at a time: a new pickup replaces the last, and each has its own colour and look.
• Pellet Burst: about 30 seconds of quick little pellet streaks that fly straight ahead and land with a small pop. A couple of pellets give a cheeky hunter a cartoon "Bonk!", then it vanishes in a puff of confetti and you get coins
• Rocket Pop (fire): about 30 homing toy rockets with fiery trails and warm orange flame bursts that bonk hunters and pop whatever they throw
• Zap Wand (lightning): about a dozen crackling orbs that zig-zag towards hunters, nets and obstacles and land with an electric pop
• Poof Toy (confetti): your bird lobs smiling toys on high and low arcs with a colourful trail; each lands in a pop of confetti that dazes hunters, pops their nets and cracks obstacles nearby
• Every obstacle cracks and crumbles under every helper, boss levels included, opening a bigger gap and giving you coins
• Witch-hunters grow tougher as the worlds go by, from pointy hats to an Archwitch, and vanish in a confetti poof. They never hurt you once dazed

9 WORLDS, LOTS OF OBSTACLES
Runs mix each world's columns with slime friends, barricades, balloon walls, toy blocks, cloud puffs, windmills, boulders and crystals. All of them pop into confetti when your helpers hit them.
• Snowy Peaks: faceted ice pillars with crystal spike tips
• Outer Space: cratered asteroid columns with glowing veins
• Coral Reef: bubbling coral columns
• Lava Land: cracked lava rock with glowing seams
• Pastel Skies: swirled pastel pillars
• Night City: neon-lit skyscrapers
• Sunset Desert, Blossom Valley and the classic Day Sky
A new world opens every 20 levels, and boss levels shake things up with spiked obstacles.

MORE TO ENJOY
• Dash, shield, shrink and ghost skills help in tight spots. The ghost can be used again and again, with a reload ring that fills as you fly
• Pause button with a 3-2-1 countdown; the game pauses itself when you leave the app
• Fly close to the edges to start a combo chain and boost your coin pickups
• Daily missions and streaks
• End-of-run reward chest, always coins only
• Weekly leaderboard and trophies to unlock

GOOD TO KNOW
• Free to play. No real-money purchases; coins are earned by playing
• Contains ads. Banner ads appear on menu and game-over screens, never during a run. Video ads are optional and only play when you choose them
• One-thumb controls, original art and music

Hop in and see how far your Phoenix can fly. How many stages can you evolve through?

## Notes for Danny
- 1.19 changes (local candidate, code 23, nothing uploaded):
  - One helper at a time. A new pickup replaces the running one, and the same kind stacks up to 2x.
  - Pickups still arrive about every 7-8 s.
  - Pellets are back to the 1.17 size.
  - Each helper fires like the rocket but has its own look and path:
    - Pellets: straight streaks with a small white pop, no sparkle.
    - Rocket Pop: the 1.18 look, unchanged, flying straight and homing.
    - Zap Wand: random zig-zag orbs with a lightning trail and an electric pop.
    - Poof Toy: random lobbed arcs with a colourful trail and a confetti pop.
  - Magnet is now a collected, timed pickup: 8 s, with a ring while it is active. The always-on built-in magnet and the evolution-card magnet line are gone.
  - The Ghost button shows no number, only a reload ring.
  - Every obstacle breaks under every helper: levels 1-2, boss spiked columns, both halves and regrown stumps.
  - Helpers now also appear on boss levels.
  - New obstacle shapes are mixed into runs (about 70% of columns), themed per world. Boss levels keep the spiked column. Every shape uses the same column hitbox as a pipe, so gaps and difficulty are unchanged.
- LEGAL:
  1. NEW cute slime "monster" obstacles (smiling blobs that bob and poof into confetti, no gore). Please do a content-rating check.
  2. Hunters are poofed away instead of only stunned (as in 1.18). The earlier approval said "stunned only".
  3. Fire (the rocket's effect; it is not a separate helper) and lightning (Zap Wand) visuals are cartoon-style and capped. Re-check the content-rating questionnaire (mild cartoon fantasy violence).
  4. Helpers now run on boss levels (the 1.18 text said they never appear there).
  5. Magnet and trail refunds carry over unchanged from 1.18. They are approximate and one-time.
