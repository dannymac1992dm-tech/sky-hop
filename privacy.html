3. Information we process

3.1 Data stored only on your device (today)

Sky Hop currently stores the following locally on your device (for example via browser localStorage or equivalent on a wrapped native build):

  • High score / best score and best level — so your best result can be shown next time you play
  • Game progress — in-game coins, shop items, birds, skins, trails, skills and upgrades, trophies, goals, daily challenge progress, and continue tokens
  • Settings — whether sound is muted, whether extras are on, and graphics quality
  • Rewarded ad counters — a simple count of rewarded ads watched, used by the game to track unlock progress

This data stays on your device. It is not uploaded to servers operated by Dany Oudeesh because the game does not use accounts, cloud save, or a publisher analytics backend.

You can typically clear this data by clearing the App’s storage / site data, or by uninstalling the App (exact steps depend on your device and browser).

3.2 Technical and device information (general)

Like most apps and websites, when Sky Hop runs on a device or is loaded in a browser, the underlying platform may expose standard technical information (for example device type, OS version, screen size, or network-related signals used by the OS or WebView). The Game itself does not require you to provide a name, email, phone number, or payment card for play.

3.3 Audio

Sky Hop uses on-device Web Audio (or equivalent) for sound effects and background music. Mute preference is stored locally as described above. Audio processing for gameplay SFX/BGM is not used to identify you.

3.4 Information we do not collect (current design)

Under the current design, Sky Hop does not:

  • Require account registration or login
  • Collect your name, postal address, or government ID for gameplay
  • Sell your personal information as a standalone product
  • Operate a publisher-run chat, social graph, or cloud leaderboard
  • Offer real-money purchases. Coins, the shop, and skills are in-game only; coins are earned by playing (and some items can be unlocked by watching optional rewarded ads)

If you use the in-game Share button, your device’s share sheet (or clipboard) is used to share a short message with your level and score. Nothing is shared unless you choose to share it.

If these practices change, this policy will be updated (see Section 13).

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

Sky Hop is a free download on Google Play. The Android app currently shows ads from Google AdMob, Google’s mobile advertising service. The free web version of the game at the hosted site does not show AdMob ads.

The App shows two kinds of ads:

  • Banner ads on the menu and game-over screens. Banners are hidden while you are playing.
  • Rewarded ads, which are always optional. A rewarded ad plays only if you tap to watch one, for example to continue a run after a crash, to double the coins from a run, or to unlock skills or items early. If you don’t want to watch one, just skip it and keep playing.

Ads in the App are limited to content suitable for a general audience.

4.1 What ads may collect or use

The Google Mobile Ads SDK in the App may collect or process information such as:

  • Device identifiers, such as the Android advertising ID (subject to platform rules and your settings)
  • IP address, which may be used to estimate your general location
  • Usage and ad interaction data (for example app launches, ads shown, taps on ads, and whether a rewarded ad was completed)
  • Device and app information (device model, OS version, app version, language)
  • Diagnostic data needed to deliver ads, measure them, and prevent fraud

This data is collected and used by Google, not by Dany Oudeesh. Exact categories depend on your consent choices, your device settings, and applicable law (for example whether personalized or non-personalized ads are shown).

4.2 Purpose of ad-related processing

Ads are used to:

  • Show advertisements in the App, which keeps the App free
  • Measure ad performance and prevent fraud / invalid traffic
  • Where you have allowed it and the law permits, personalize ads based on interests or activity

4.3 Google’s policies

Google processes ad-related data under its own privacy policy. To learn how Google uses information from apps that use its services, see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services (https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites). Google’s Privacy Policy is at https://policies.google.com/privacy.

Sky Hop does not use other ad networks or mediation partners.

4.4 Consent message

Sky Hop uses Google’s consent tool (the User Messaging Platform). When the App starts, it checks with Google whether a consent message is required where you live. If it is (for example in the European Economic Area, the UK, and Switzerland), the App shows Google’s consent message, where you can choose whether to allow personalized ads and related data use. Depending on your choices and region, Google may show non-personalized ads, limited ads, or no ads.

The App’s consent setup also covers privacy laws in certain US states, which give residents the right to opt out of the “sale” or “sharing” of personal information for targeted advertising. The App does not currently show a separate in-app opt-out screen for these laws; you can limit targeted ads by deleting or resetting your advertising ID and managing ad personalization as described in Section 4.5.

4.5 Changing your consent and other ad controls

  • Change your consent choice: The App does not currently have an in-app button to reopen the consent message. To make a new choice, clear Sky Hop’s app data (on most Android devices: Settings > Apps > Sky Hop > Storage > Clear storage) or uninstall and reinstall the App. Where the consent message applies in your region, it will be shown again the next time you open the App. Note that clearing app data also deletes your local game progress.
  • Reset or delete your advertising ID: On most Android devices, go to Settings > Google > Ads (on some devices, Settings > Privacy > Ads). There you can reset your advertising ID, or on newer Android versions delete it, which stops apps from using it for personalized ads.
  • Opt out of personalized ads more generally: You can manage Google’s ad personalization at https://myadcenter.google.com/.
  • Skip rewarded ads: Rewarded ads are optional. You never have to watch one to keep playing.
