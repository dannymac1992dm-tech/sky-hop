3. Information we process

3.1 Data stored only on your device (today)

Sky Hop currently stores the following locally on your device (for example via browser localStorage or equivalent on a wrapped native build):

  • High score / best score — so your best result can be shown next time you play
  • Mute preference — whether sound effects and music are muted

This data stays on your device. It is not uploaded to servers operated by Danny Oudeesh because the game does not use accounts, cloud save, or a publisher analytics backend for these preferences.

You can typically clear this data by clearing the App’s storage / site data, or by uninstalling the App (exact steps depend on your device and browser).

3.2 Technical and device information (general)

Like most apps and websites, when Sky Hop runs on a device or is loaded in a browser, the underlying platform may expose standard technical information (for example device type, OS version, screen size, or network-related signals used by the OS or WebView). The Game itself does not require you to provide a name, email, phone number, or payment card for play.

3.3 Audio

Sky Hop uses on-device Web Audio (or equivalent) for sound effects and background music. Mute preference is stored locally as described above. Audio processing for gameplay SFX/BGM is not used to identify you.

3.4 Information we do not collect (current design)

Under the current design, Sky Hop does not:

  • Require account registration or login
  • Collect your name, postal address, or government ID for gameplay
  • Sell your personal information as a standalone product
  • Operate a publisher-run chat, social graph, or cloud leaderboard

If these practices change, this policy will be updated (see Section 10).

4. Advertising (Google AdMob or similar)

Sky Hop is planned as a free download monetized with ads after packaging (for example with Capacitor) for Google Play (Android first), and possibly other stores later.

When advertising SDKs such as Google AdMob (or a similar ad network) are integrated:

4.1 What ads may collect or use

Advertising partners may collect or process information such as:

  • Advertising identifiers (for example Android Advertising ID / similar identifiers, subject to platform rules and your settings)
  • Device and app information (device model, OS version, app version, language, time zone)
  • IP address and approximate location derived from network signals
  • Ad interaction data (impressions, clicks, and whether ads were shown)
  • Diagnostic or crash-related data needed to deliver and measure ads

Exact categories depend on the SDK configuration (for example personalized vs. non-personalized ads), your device settings, and applicable law (including consent where required).

4.2 Purpose of ad-related processing

Ads are used to:

  • Show advertisements in the App
  • Measure ad performance and prevent fraud / invalid traffic
  • Where allowed and configured, personalize ads based on interests or activity

4.3 Third-party privacy policies

Ad partners process data under their own privacy policies. For Google advertising products, review Google’s policies and disclosures, including materials for AdMob / Google Play ads. Links and product names may change; check Google’s current documentation when you publish.

7. Children’s privacy (COPPA and similar rules)

Sky Hop is intended for a general audience. It is not marketed as a kids-only or under-13 product unless Danny Oudeesh later designates it as such in store listings and age ratings.

Important note: If the Game is directed to children under 13 (or otherwise treated as a child-directed app under COPPA or similar laws), advertising and data practices must change. In that case, the publisher must:

  • Use child-directed / family-safe ad configurations where available
  • Avoid unauthorized collection of personal information from children
  • Obtain verifiable parental consent where legally required
  • Update this Privacy Policy, store Data safety disclosures, and AdMob / store settings accordingly

Until those changes are made and disclosed, do not treat Sky Hop as a COPPA-compliant child-directed product solely based on this draft.

8. Your rights and choices

Depending on where you live (for example EEA, UK, California, and other regions), you may have rights to:

  • Access, correct, or delete personal information
  • Object to or restrict certain processing
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent
  • Lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority

How to exercise rights: Contact [CONTACT EMAIL] or [SUPPORT EMAIL]. We may need to verify your request and may decline requests that are unlawful, abusive, or cannot be authenticated.
